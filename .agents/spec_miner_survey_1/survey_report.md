#### Panel 2: First-Person Drone Vision & Dense Optical Flow
- **Generator**: `draw_optical_flow_vectors(rgb_img, flow, target_size=(390, 390), step=16)` in `evaluate_and_visualize.py`.
- **Super-Resolution Upscaling**:
  - Raw Camera RGB frame ($84 \times 84 \times 3$) is upscaled to $390 \times 390$ using Bicubic Interpolation (`cv2.INTER_CUBIC`) and converted to BGR.
  - Optical Flow field ($84 \times 84 \times 2$) components $u$ and $v$ are independently resized to $390 \times 390$ with `cv2.INTER_CUBIC` and scaled by the spatial expansion factor:
    $$\text{scale}_x = \frac{390}{84} \approx 4.6428, \quad \text{scale}_y = \frac{390}{84} \approx 4.6428$$
    $$\text{flow}_x^{\text{HD}} = \text{resize}(u) \times \text{scale}_x, \quad \text{flow}_y^{\text{HD}} = \text{resize}(v) \times \text{scale}_y$$
- **Vector Sampling Grid**:
  - Regular grid evaluated at intervals of `step = 16` pixels, offset by `step // 2 = 8` px: $x, y \in \{8, 24, 40, \dots, 376\}$.
- **Motion Thresholding & Directional Color Coding**:
  - Flow magnitude: $\text{mag} = \sqrt{dx^2 + dy^2}$.
  - Noise Filter: Motion vectors with $\text{mag} \le 1.2$ pixels are suppressed.
  - Vector Magnitude Color Map:
    - Low Motion / Steady Flight ($1.2 < \text{mag} < 4.0$): Cyan BGR `(255, 255, 0)`.
    - Moderate Motion / Active Translation ($4.0 \le \text{mag} < 10.0$): Lime Green BGR `(0, 255, 120)`.
    - High Motion / Rapid Looming or Obstacle Proximity ($\text{mag} \ge 10.0$): Gold/Yellow BGR `(0, 200, 255)`.
- **Vector Projection**:
  - Endpoint: $(x_2, y_2) = (x_1 + 2.0 \cdot dx, y_1 + 2.0 \cdot dy)$.
  - Line stroke: `cv2.arrowedLine` with anti-aliasing (`cv2.LINE_AA`), thickness 1, tipLength $0.35$.
- **Footer Telemetry**: Title: `"2. DRONE VISION & OPTICAL FLOW"`, Metric: `"FPS: {fps:.1f} | Latency: {latency_ms:.2f} ms"` in Neon Lime BGR `(0, 255, 120)`.

#### Panel 3: Real-Time Saliency Heatmap (XAI Obstacle Focus)
- **Generator**: `DroneXAIEngine.generate_heatmap_overlay(...)` in `explainability.py`.
- **Attribution Methodology**:
  - Captum `Saliency` (Vanilla Gradient) for real-time inference (target action index 0, forward velocity):
    $$A_{\text{raw}} = \left| \frac{\partial \pi(s)_0}{\partial \mathbf{I}_{\text{visual}}} \right|$$
  - Captum `IntegratedGradients` ($N=30$ Riemann steps) for offline high-fidelity evaluation:
    $$\text{IG}_i(x) = (x_i - x_i') \times \int_{0}^{1} \frac{\partial F(x' + \alpha(x - x'))}{\partial x_i} d\alpha$$
  - Channel Pooling: Max-pooling across the 5 input channels ($3\text{ RGB} + 2\text{ Flow}$):
    $$A_{\text{2D}}(x, y) = \max_{c \in [0..4]} |A(c, x, y)|$$
  - Mathematical Fallback (when PyTorch autograd is unavailable):
    $$A_{\text{2D}} = |\operatorname{Sobel}_x(I)| + |\operatorname{Sobel}_y(I)| + 1.5 \cdot \sqrt{u^2 + v^2}$$
  - Dynamic Range Normalization: Min-Max normalization mapping values to $[0.0, 1.0]$.
- **Thermal Vision Rendering Pipeline**:
  1. **Bicubic Upscaling**: 2D attribution map resized to $390 \times 390$ with `cv2.INTER_CUBIC`.
  2. **Gaussian Spatial Smoothing**: Filtered with kernel size $(13, 13)$ and standard deviation $\sigma = 3.0$ to eliminate pixelation and create smooth energy gradients.
  3. **Re-Normalization**: Dynamic re-scaling to $[0.0, 1.0]$ after Gaussian smoothing.
  4. **Non-Linear Gamma Contrast Boost**:
     $$A_{\text{boosted}} = (A_{\text{smooth}})^{0.7}$$
     A gamma exponent of $0.7$ boosts subtle feature responses and broadens the perceptual thermal dynamic range.
  5. **Google TURBO Colormap**: Converted to 8-bit integer and passed through `cv2.applyColorMap(..., cv2.COLORMAP_TURBO)`.
  6. **Adaptive Alpha Blending**:
     $$\alpha(x, y) = \operatorname{clip}\left(\frac{A_{\text{boosted}}(x, y) - 0.10}{0.85}, 0.0, 1.0\right) \times \alpha_{\text{max}}$$
     With $\alpha_{\text{max}} = 0.65$ or $0.75$. Unsalient background regions remain completely transparent, allowing the RGB environment to show through, while obstacles glow brightly with thermal colors.
  7. **Neon Peak-Saliency Contours**:
     $$\mathcal{M}_{\text{peak}} = \left(A_{\text{boosted}} > 0.65\right)$$
     External boundaries extracted via `cv2.findContours(..., cv2.RETR_EXTERNAL, cv2.CHAIN_APPROX_SIMPLE)` and drawn in Neon Cyan/Gold BGR `(255, 255, 0)` with thickness 1 and anti-aliasing (`cv2.LINE_AA`).
- **Footer Telemetry**: Title: `"3. XAI SALIENCY HEATMAP"`, Metric: `"Spatial IoU Fidelity: {fidelity_iou:.3f}"` in Golden Amber BGR `(0, 200, 255)`.

### 2.4 Test Scenario Evaluation Logic & Metrics

Implemented in `evaluate_policy(args)`:
- **Evaluation Protocols**:
  - `--eval-mode training`: Evaluates on 5 seeds used during training (`[42, 142, 242, 342, 442]`). Establishes empirical upper-bound policy performance.
  - `--eval-mode unseen`: Evaluates on 5 completely unseen procedural seeds (`[101, 202, 303, 404, 505]`). Tests generalization and obstacle avoidance in unfamiliar environments.
  - `--eval-mode all`: Evaluates on all 9 seeds (`[42, 142, 242, 342, 101, 202, 303, 404, 505]`).
- **Episode Termination Conditions**:
  - Goal Reached: Euclidean distance to goal $d < 0.8$ m. Terminal reward bonus $+100.0$.
  - Collision: PyBullet contact points with obstacles/walls $> 0$, or out-of-bounds boundary violation ($x \notin [-4.6, 4.6]$, $y \notin [-9.6, 9.6]$, $z \notin [0.15, 3.5]$). Terminal penalty $-100.0$.
  - Truncation / Timeout: Step count $\ge$ `max_steps` (default 500).
- **Explanation Fidelity Metric (Spatial IoU)**:
  $$\text{Spatial IoU} = \frac{|\mathcal{M}_{\text{attr}} \cap \mathcal{M}_{\text{depth}}|}{|\mathcal{M}_{\text{attr}} \cup \mathcal{M}_{\text{depth}}| + 10^{-6}}$$
  Where $\mathcal{M}_{\text{attr}} = (A_{\text{2D}} \ge \text{Percentile}_{85}(A_{\text{2D}}))$ and $\mathcal{M}_{\text{depth}} = (D_{\text{ground\_truth}} < 1.5\text{ m})$.
- **Final Summary Statistics**:
  - Mean Episode Reward ($\pm$ standard deviation).
  - Success Rate (% goal reached).
  - Collision Rate (% obstacle/wall collisions).
  - Mean Explanation Fidelity Spatial IoU.
  - Mean Decision Latency (ms) and effective FPS.

### 3.2 `overnight_train.bat` Automation & Shutdown Architecture
Designed for unattended multi-hour deep reinforcement learning sessions with fail-safes.

- **Session Configuration**:
  - Color Theme: `COLOR 0B` (Bright Cyan on Black).
  - Target Duration: $\approx 4$ hours.
  - Total Environment Steps: $4,000,000$ timesteps.
  - Parallel Environments: $16$ workers using `SubprocVecEnv`.
  - Rollout Horizon: $512$ steps per worker ($8,192$ transitions per batch).
  - Mini-Batch Size: $2,048$. PPO Epochs: $10$.
  - Live Dashboard: Enabled via `--dashboard`.
- **Pre-Flight Checkpoint Verification**:
  - Checks if `best_model.pt` exists in workspace:
    `if exist "best_model.pt" goto LAUNCH_SERVICES`
  - If checkpoint is missing, automatically runs BC pre-training before proceeding:
    `python pretrain_bc.py --n-demo-envs 300 --target-demo-steps 80000 --bc-epochs 60`
- **Asynchronous Service Launching Sequence**:
  - **Step 1/3**: Spawns TensorBoard server minimized in background:
    `start "TensorBoard Web Monitor" /min cmd /c "python -m tensorboard.main --logdir runs"`
  - Delays 3 seconds to permit server initialization: `ping 127.0.0.1 -n 3 > nul`.
  - Opens local browser to dashboard: `start "" "http://localhost:6006"`.
  - **Step 2/3**: Executes 4M timestep PPO training pipeline:
    `python train.py --total-timesteps 4000000 --n-envs 16 --n-steps 512 --batch-size 2048 --update-epochs 10 --dashboard`
  - **Step 3/3**: Automated Windows Shutdown Sequence:
    - Initiates forced system shutdown countdown with 60-second grace period:
      `shutdown /s /f /t 60 /c "Overnight Autonomous Drone PPO Training Complete! Computer shutting down."`
    - Provides interactive cancellation trap: Script pauses; if user presses any key, executes `shutdown /a` (abort shutdown) and keeps workstation active.

### 3.3 Hardware Acceleration & Environment Configurations

- **NVIDIA Blackwell (`sm_120`) & RTX 50-Series Support**:
  - PyTorch 2.5+ / Nightly build compiled against CUDA 12.8 wheel (`cu128`).
  - Strict CUDA detection in `get_device()`: verifies `torch.cuda.is_available()`, extracts compute capability string (`sm_120`), and prints GPU VRAM capacity.
  - Accelerations enabled in PyTorch:
    - `torch.backends.cudnn.benchmark = True`: Auto-tunes cuDNN convolution kernels for fixed $84 \times 84$ input geometry.
    - `torch.backends.cuda.matmul.allow_tf32 = True`: Enables TensorFloat-32 tensor cores for matrix multiplications.
    - `torch.backends.cudnn.allow_tf32 = True`: Enables TF32 for cuDNN convolutions.
    - `torch.amp.autocast(device_type="cuda", dtype=torch.float16)`: Automatic Mixed Precision FP16 training with gradient scaling (`torch.cuda.amp.GradScaler`).
    - `torch.compile(model, mode="reduce-overhead")`: Kernel fusion and CUDA Graph capture reducing host-to-device dispatch overhead.
- **Headless EGL & PyBullet Rendering Engine**:
  - Environment variable `PYBULLET_EGL=1` activates PyBullet's hardware EGL plugin on Linux/headless servers.
  - On Windows systems where EGL or OpenGL GUI display is unconfigured:
    - Primary renderer: PyBullet Tiny Renderer `p.ER_TINY_RENDERER`.
    - Pure-Python / NumPy Fallback: Built-in 3D Perspective Raycasting Engine (`environment.py` lines 220–292) calculating floor tile intersections and cylindrical/cuboid obstacle ray intersections with distance shading, ensuring zero-crash headless execution.

## 7. Edge Cases & Boundary Conditions

| # | Feature | Input / Condition | Observed / Documented Behavior |
|---|---------|-------------------|--------------------------------|
| 1 | GUI Window Display | Headless server or missing Win32 display server | `cv2.namedWindow` throws exception; caught by `try...except`, sets `args.headless = True`, and continues execution headlessly without crashing. |
| 2 | Model Checkpoint Loading | `best_model.pt` missing when running evaluation | `os.path.exists` check detects missing file; logs warning `Checkpoint not found! Using random weights.` and runs evaluation with uninitialized model. |
| 3 | PyTorch Import Failure | PyTorch not installed or blocked by Windows AppLocker | `TORCH_AVAILABLE = False`. `model.py` falls back to CPU NumPy linear algebra implementation; `explainability.py` falls back to Sobel edge + optical flow attribution. |
| 4 | Saliency Attribution Smoothing | Flat or constant attribution map (zero dynamic range) | Dynamic range check `if attr_max - attr_min > 1e-8:` prevents division by zero; outputs zeroed attribution array. |
| 5 | Spatial IoU Union Calculation | Zero salient pixels and zero obstacles in frame | Union equals zero: `if union == 0:` branch returns `1.0` if intersection is zero (perfect explanation of empty space), else `0.0`. |
| 6 | Optical Flow Stationary Drone | Drone perfectly stationary (zero pixel displacement) | Flow vector magnitude is below threshold $1.2$; loop continues without drawing any arrows, leaving raw camera frame clean. |
| 7 | Overnight Training Missing Weights | `best_model.pt` does not exist when `overnight_train.bat` starts | Script detects absence via `if exist`, pauses PPO launch, runs `pretrain_bc.py` for 5 minutes to generate baseline weights, then automatically resumes PPO. |
| 8 | Auto-Shutdown Abort | User presses any key during the 60-second shutdown countdown | Script proceeds past `pause` and executes `shutdown /a`, successfully canceling the pending Windows shutdown command and keeping PC powered. |
| 9 | Menu Invalid Input | User inputs character other than 0-6 in `run_project.bat` | Command file catches invalid token, displays `"Invalid selection! Please try again."`, prompts for pause, and loops back to `:MENU`. |
| 10 | Drone Out of Bounds | Drone velocity pushes quadrotor beyond arena boundaries | `_clamp_to_arena` clamps coordinates to hard limits ($[-4.6, 4.6]$, $[-9.6, 9.6]$, $[0.15, 3.5]$), zeroes velocities, sets `self.out_of_bounds = True`, triggering collision penalty $-100.0$ and episode termination. |
